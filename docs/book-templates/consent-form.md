# Consent form

*Appendix 14.C of* **Introduction to Arabic Speech Technology** *by Hend S. Al-Khalifa. Copy it, fill it in, and publish it with your data.*

## Consent form (record and adapt to local law)

- Statement that the session records the participant's voice.
- The specific permitted uses, including model training and any redistribution.
- A clause prohibiting attempts to identify individual speakers from the data.
- Retention period and the participant's right to withdraw.
- Contact for questions and the participant's signature and date.

## Example: the case study’s consent form, as the speaker reads it

- One page, in the speaker’s own variety of Arabic as well as in English. The wording below is an example and is not legal advice: have the final text checked against the law that applies where you record.
- **What is recorded:** this session records your voice while you read a list of short commands and a few ordinary sentences. Nothing else about you is recorded, and no video is taken.
- **What it will be used for:** to train and test speech recognition systems, and to publish the recordings for other researchers to use for non-commercial research. Your recordings may be redistributed as part of the corpus.
- **What may not be done with it:** no one who receives the corpus may attempt to identify you from your voice, or link these recordings to you. This restriction applies to everyone who receives the data.
- **What is kept, and for how long:** the audio and a code number are kept indefinitely as part of the published corpus. The sheet linking that code number to your name is destroyed after twelve months and is never published.
- **Your right to withdraw:** you may withdraw at any time before publication, and your recordings will be deleted. After publication, copies already distributed cannot be recalled, which is why this form asks you to agree to publication now rather than later.
- **Questions:** the contact name, address and telephone number of the person responsible for the project.
- Signature and date, with a copy of this page given to the participant.
