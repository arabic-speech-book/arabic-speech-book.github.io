# About the Book

**Foundations of Arabic Speech Processing: A Modern Introduction** is a comprehensive textbook that bridges traditional speech-processing methods with advanced artificial-intelligence approaches to address the unique challenges of Arabic. It serves researchers, graduate students, and industry professionals by offering a unified framework for understanding and developing Arabic speech technologies.

The book begins with foundational concepts in both speech processing and Arabic linguistics, then explores acoustic and language modeling techniques adapted specifically for Arabic — from classical statistical methods to modern neural architectures.

Special attention is given to the linguistic diversity of Arabic: its complex morphology, dialectal variation, and frequent code-switching. The book examines recent advances in speech foundation models and large language models such as Whisper, emphasizing their transformative impact on Arabic speech recognition, synthesis, and understanding. Practical case studies and system evaluations illustrate real-world applications, while ethical considerations and future research directions encourage responsible innovation.

## What makes it different

- **Theory + practice** — every concept is paired with a runnable [notebook](notebooks/index.md).
- **Arabic-first** — linguistics, datasets, and evaluation are framed around Arabic, not bolted on.
- **Modern** — foundation models, self-supervised learning, and LLM-based speech are first-class topics.
