# Conferences, Journals & Community

Where speech research — Arabic and general — is published, presented, and built. The venues and bodies that regularly carry Arabic speech work are marked, alongside the major global ones and the labs and companies shaping the field.

!!! note "This page changes — check before you cite"
    Conference homepages rotate by year, labs reorganize, and companies come and go. *Last checked: 2026-08-12.* For open-source toolkits (SpeechBrain, ESPnet, NeMo, Kaldi, Coqui) see the [Tools](tools.md) page; for corpora and models see the [Resource Index](resources/resource-index.md).
